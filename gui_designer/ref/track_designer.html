The dashed rectangle is the smaller area of LSY's real room; the simulator allows the whole 6 m box inside the red line. The teal line is a sketch of the route, not the path the drone will fly.

3D view: drag to look around, wheel to zoom.

Side view: height against x

Drag the numbered round handle of a gate (or its bar) up or down to set the height of its center, and sideways to move it along x. The top of a pole, a route point and the end pad work the same way. The wheel over a selected object changes its height by 0.05 m.
