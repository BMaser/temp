Where the battery and stack go — two frame options

Both sketches are side section + plan at the same scale (1 px = 0.5 mm). Grey = chassis, blue = 2S 850 mAh pack (70 × 35 × 20 mm), green = 30.5 mm FC + ESC stack, dark = EDF duct. The EDF axis is dashed red; the quartered circle is the estimated center of gravity from part masses (plate, EDF, pack, stack, pods, camera). Both come out slightly aft because the pack sits behind the duct — sliding the pack forward against the duct wall or moving the stack aft brings it onto the axis.

1aScale the frame up · 250 mm class
Pros single flat plate, pack lies flat behind the duct, EDF 64 mm gives real lift margin, cool airflow around the stack. Cons 2.5× the span (≈330 mm motor-to-motor), heavier, whole model and simulator mass rescale.
1bKeep the size · add a belly bay
Pros footprint and simulator mass stay as they are, pods fit a 20 × 20 stack and a pack mounted across the tail. Cons the 70 mm pack overhangs the 60 mm body by 5 mm each side, drone is 30 mm tall, pack hangs behind the exhaust so CG needs the stack and camera forward to balance; only a 1S–2S small pack is realistic.
