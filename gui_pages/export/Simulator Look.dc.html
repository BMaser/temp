Drone notes

Sports-car stance: low wide chassis, dark cabin glass, white nose plate and lens at the front, black rear wing at the back. 138 × 134 × 36 mm — inside the 140 × 140 × 40 mm box.

Five rotors: four 40 mm props in guard rings on swept arms (stability and steering) and a 56 mm ducted rotor on the roof, set back of center (main lifter).

Front ≠ back from every side: white nose and white front props vs. black wing, black rear props and the raised duct behind the cabin.

Camera cone kept clear: nothing ahead of x = 30 mm rises above z = −4 mm; the front guard rings only touch the upper corners of the picture, as today.
